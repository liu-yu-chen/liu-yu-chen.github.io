## Project question

What do consumers notice about new-energy vehicles, and how do stated purchase intentions vary across respondents in Xiamen? The project combined online-text analysis with a local questionnaire and consumer segmentation.

## My contribution

The supplied team report lists my responsibilities as online-text mining, random-forest feature analysis, questionnaire design, clustering, and LDA topic modeling.

## Data and methods

The report describes **8,058 online comments** and a stratified, multistage Xiamen survey with **368 valid responses from 403 distributed questionnaires** (91.32% valid returns). The questionnaire reports **Cronbach's α = 0.895** and **KMO = 0.715**.

LDA topics covered range, charging convenience, battery safety, driving experience, price, and policy support. In the fitted random forest, the report lists importance values of **0.297 for occupation, 0.272 for income, 0.172 for education, 0.166 for age, and 0.091 for gender**.

## Reading the findings carefully

In the report's classification of online comments, 50.0% were positive, 24.2% neutral, and 25.8% negative. The model's feature-importance scores are descriptive of this project; they are not causal effects or estimates of purchase probabilities in all Xiamen residents.

The methods discussion says 290 current or prospective buyers were selected for a five-cluster analysis. However, the reported profile table gives cluster sizes of **91, 40, 14, 103, and 17**, which sum to **265**. The report does not reconcile the remaining 25 cases, so these cluster sizes are not graphed as complete coverage. Survey responses and online comments may also differ from the broader Xiamen population.
